A. Cost and profit Recommended

One line, the answer in green or red. What it sells for is in the hover.

  • Short: fits beside long reagent names, even with gold amounts.
  • Same place as today, nothing in Blizzard's window moves.
  • To see the selling price you hover, as you do now for each material's price.

B. Cost, selling price and profit

The whole sum on one line, like the bottom bar of a recipe search.

C. Two lines

Cost on the first line, selling price and profit under it.

  • Easy to read, each line short.
  • Does not fit: on recipes with optional reagents, the second line runs into Blizzard's "Optional Reagents" heading. Fixing that means moving part of Blizzard's window, which aux avoids.

The sum behind the number

The made item has no price yet (never seen at the auction house). Gray, and the hover says to search for it.

Materials  2s 15c

The recipe makes no item (an enchant). Cost only, as today.

Where the numbers come from

  • Made item's price: its latest price, the same number as Value in its tooltip, from saved prices. No auction house request, so it works anywhere in the world, like the cost.
  • The cut: 5% of the selling price, the same as aux uses everywhere. Outside Not yet checked on an in-game invoice.
  • The deposit is left out: it comes back when the item sells. A craft listed several times before it sells costs a deposit each time; aux cannot know that ahead.
  • Materials: unchanged: each at its latest price, or a vendor's when a vendor sells it for less.
