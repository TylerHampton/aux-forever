Option A: 4th Search sub-tab (Recommended)

Recommended
  • Zero clutter on normal searches: Search Results stays clean for regular shopping.
  • Full room for columns: Shows Required, Have (with alt hover breakdown), Need, Best Source (AH vs Vendor), Unit Price, and multi-tier Total Cost cleanly even at the 1000x549 minimum window.
  • 1-click buy loop: Buy Bar pre-fills Need and auto-advances to the next unbought AH material after each purchase.
  • Grows naturally: Craftsman's Writs and Waylaid Crates can open straight into the same Shopping List later.

Option B: Compact strip over Search Results

  • Pros: Lets you see every individual auction price tier underneath without switching sub-tabs.
  • Cons: Squeezes the table height on 1000x549 windows, hides per-material cost/alt breakdown inside hover chips, and makes vendor-cheaper items harder to read at a glance.
  • Note: In Option A, right-clicking any material row jumps straight to its full listings in Search Results anyway (matching docs/clicks.md).
