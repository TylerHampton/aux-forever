# Review: Sniper deal logic in 0.8.2 (H-007)

- **Date:** 2026-11-09 (Tyler's time)
- **Branch:** `claude/magical-maxwell-eyud0b`
- **Commit reviewed:** `219db5f` (`fddd029..219db5f`, Sniper changes and `scan.is_full_scan`)

## Verdict

The core direction of 0.8.2 is sound: judging flips against `min(usual, market)`, reading `market_now` from the candidate's own listings with zero extra requests, pre-filtering the browse list so a flat market does not trigger 1,792 opens, skipping a redundant re-read on a fresh click, and holding Sniper rounds while a Full scan runs all address the real failure modes in FB-036, FB-037, and FB-028.

However, there are **eight logic and performance oversights** across those changes that will either let false deals through, hide genuine snipes, or break second-unit buying in the next round.

## Answers to the five questions in H-007

1. **Is "the lower of the usual price and today's market" the right reference for a flip, or is there a better practice?**
   - **Yes, `min(usual, market)` is the right reference.** In TradeSkillMaster (`[Outside]`), standard sniper formulas cap the reference price at `min(DBMarket, DBHistorical)` for the same reason: when today's listings are temporarily high or thin, `usual` prevents buying an overpriced listing; when a market has fallen or flooded with supply below its multi-day median, `market` prevents buying into a wall you cannot resell above after the 5% cut (`[Outside]`, `AUCTION_CUT = .05`).
   - **Where I disagree (Finding 7):** When `market < usual` (`reason == 'market'`), the Sniper row computes `Why` (`30% of market`) and `Profit each` (`6s 50c`) from `market` (`10s`), while the `Usual` column still shows `22s` and `market` (`10s`) is not shown anywhere on screen.

2. **`market_now`: do the cheapest quarter, the 3-seller rule and one unit per other version hide real deals or let false ones through?**
   - **Yes to all three (Findings 1, 2, and 3), with concrete listings below:**
     - **3-seller rule (Finding 1):** Because `if total > 0` runs and returns before `if sellers >= MARKET_SELLERS`, the 3-seller rule is dead code whenever even 1 overpriced unit exists above `cheapest` (or when `other_versions` is non-empty). Concrete false deal: `{rec(210, 80, {seller_count = 6}), rec(2200, 1)}` returns `2200` instead of `210`.
     - **Cheapest quarter (Finding 2):** Because `others` has no minimum unit or seller floor, two cheap units 1 copper apart with nothing else listed (`{rec(209, 1), rec(210, 1)}`, `usual = 2200`) set `market = 210` and hide the deal, whereas 80 units at one price (`{rec(210, 80)}`) or adding 100 expensive units (`{rec(209, 1), rec(210, 1), rec(2200, 100)}`) is a deal.
     - **One unit per other version (Finding 3):** Ignoring `other.totalQuantity`, `other.containsOwnerItem`, and whether `other.minPrice` is a lone auction or bid both hides real deals (two versions with 1 cheap auction each at `10s` and `11s` mutually block each other in `check_list` so neither opens) and lets false deals through (1 version at `6s` with 8 overpriced auctions at `32s` outvotes another version's 78 auctions at `13s` because the 78 auctions count as `units = 1`).

3. **The list check (point 3): is the trade-off acceptable, and is there a cheaper or safer way to cut the 1,792 opens?**
   - **Filtering on the browse list is the right approach, but using `history.latest` with `age == 0` causes self-poisoning and a much wider trade-off than "an auction alone" (Finding 4):**
     - `history.latest` returns `record.market or record.low`, so an item with no complete look today (`record.market == nil`) still returns today's single lowest price (`record.low`) with `age == 0`.
     - When `check_item` opens a genuine deal (`Lone Sword` at `3s` or `Dumped Ore` at `2s 10c`), `scan.read_item` calls `record_view`, which writes that deal price into `record.market` (`history.latest` becomes `3s`, `age = 0`), blocking the next round from finding a second auction at `3s` or the rest of the `Dumped Ore` after a purchase.
     - Because `history.market_price` uses `MARKET_SHARE = .05` (`ceil(ceil(total * 0.05) / 2)`), `middle == 1` for every item with `total <= 40` units. Thus 1 snipe at `3s` alongside 39 normal auctions at `32s` in today's Full scan sets `record.market = 3s`, not only a lone auction. And for a truly lone auction (`result.totalQuantity == 1`), skipping the today's-scan ceiling when `result.totalQuantity <= 1` avoids the lone-auction trade-off completely.

4. **`FRESH_SECONDS` and the 15-minute market memory: any case where they buy the wrong thing, spend more than shown, or keep a stale market too long?**
   - **Never spends more than shown:** `buy_bar` cancels any commodity quote above `current.estimate`, and `PlaceBid` sends the exact `buyout_price` for that `auction_id` (`[Code]`).
   - **Keeps a stale market too long (Finding 5):** When the cheap supply wall (`87c`) sells out and `result.minPrice` on the browse list jumps back to `22s`, `check_list` leaves `markets[key].market = 87` in place for 15 minutes; if a real snipe (`55c`) is listed 2 minutes later, it is rejected against the vanished `87c` wall. Conversely, when `check_list` rejects an item because of a temporary `other_versions` or `markets[key]` entry, it writes `known[key] = price`, which never expires while `price` stays the same.
   - **Can fail to buy a gear deal (Finding 6):** In 0.4.1 (`[Seen]`, `tabs/sniper/core.lua:615-619`), `PlaceBid` on a Sniper gear deal did nothing after the Sniper searched other items. `FRESH_SECONDS = 15` checks only elapsed time, not whether another candidate in the same round or the next round (`ROUND_PAUSE = 2.5`) ran a search after `deal_read_at`.

5. **Anything in these five changes that breaks a rule above or a test's intent:**
   - **Finding 4** breaks the next-round re-check promised in `on_success` (`tabs/sniper/core.lua:717-718`) and `on_buy` (`core.lua:759-760`).
   - **Finding 6** bypasses the 0.4.1 gear re-read (`tests/load_test.lua:2264-2304`) whenever another item is searched within 15 seconds of the gear deal.
   - **Finding 8** runs `info.item_key_price_key` and `coro_wait` across all ~7,700 browse results on every round in a separate pre-pass (`tabs/sniper/core.lua:345-363`), even when `known[key] == price` and even for the ~6,000+ items whose `itemID` appears only once on the list.

## Numbered Findings (ordered most serious first)

### 1. `sellers >= MARKET_SELLERS` in `market_now` is dead code whenever any listing exists above `cheapest`, letting false deals through

- **What:** In `auxForever/tabs/sniper/core.lua:116-149`, `M.market_now` puts only records with `unit > cheapest` into `others` (`total`) and counts `sellers` only for `unit == cheapest`. Then `if total > 0` (line 135) runs and returns `other.price` before `if sellers >= MARKET_SELLERS then return cheapest end` (line 146) is ever reached.
- **Where:** `auxForever/tabs/sniper/core.lua:116-149` (`M.market_now`)
- **Why it matters to a player:** On a live auction house, almost every commodity or gear item has at least one old or overpriced listing above the cheapest price.
  - **Concrete listing 1:** `Flat Ore` (`usual = 22s` / `2200`) has `{rec(210, 80, {seller_count = 6}), rec(2200, 1)}` (80 units from 6 sellers at `2s 10c`, and 1 unit from 1 seller at `22s`). Because all 80 units are at `cheapest` (`210`), they are excluded from `others`. `others` contains only `{price = 2200, units = 1}` (`total = 1 > 0`), so `market_now` returns `2200` (`22s`) and never reaches line 146. The Sniper flags the 6-seller wall at `2s 10c` as a deal (`10% of usual`) and offers all 80 units on the buy bar.
  - **Concrete listing 2:** An herb (`usual = 22s`) has `{rec(85, 1), rec(87, 200, {seller_count = 5}), rec(2200, 5)}`. While the 1 unit at `85c` is up, `87c` is in `others` and `market_now` returns `87c` (no deal). As soon as the 1 unit at `85c` is bought, `87c` becomes `cheapest`, all 200 units at `87c` are excluded from `others`, `market_now` jumps from `87c` to `2200` (`22s`), and the Sniper alerts on the 200-unit wall at `87c` as a deal.
  - **Fix:** Check `if sellers >= MARKET_SELLERS then return cheapest end` *before* `if total > 0` (if 3 or more independent sellers are already asking `cheapest`, `cheapest` is already a multi-seller market price regardless of higher listings).
- **How sure:** Fact from the addon code (`tabs/sniper/core.lua:116-149`).

### 2. `check_list` uses `history.latest` (`age == 0`), which includes single-auction `record.low`, self-poisons after `scan.read_item`, and treats 1 cheap unit out of 40 in a Full scan as the market

- **What:** In `auxForever/tabs/sniper/core.lua:388-391`, `check_list` calls `local latest, age = history.latest(price_key)` and, when `age == 0`, clamps `market = market and min(market, latest) or latest`.
- **Where:**
  - `auxForever/tabs/sniper/core.lua:255-279, 386-391, 639-647, 717-718, 759-760`
  - `auxForever/core/scan.lua:520-532, 565-579, 675-682`
  - `auxForever/core/history.lua:155-157, 227, 241-254, 321-323, 381-387`
- **Why it matters to a player:**
  1. **Self-poisoning after `scan.read_item` breaks buying a second auction or remaining stack and breaks `settings_changed()`:**
     - When `check_item` opens a candidate (`Lone Sword` at `300c` or `Dumped Ore` with 80 units from 1 seller at `210c`, `usual = 2200c`), `scan.read_item` (`core/scan.lua:675-682`) runs `history.process_auction` (setting `record.low = 300`) and `record_view` (setting `record.market = 300, record.units = 1` for `Lone Sword`, or `record.market = 210, record.units = 80` for `Dumped Ore`).
     - Even for gear with a random ending where `record_view` returns early (`core/scan.lua:528`), `history.process_auction` sets `record.low = 300`, and `history.latest` (`core/history.lua:156, 323`) returns `day_price(record)` which is `record.market or record.low` (`300`, with `age = 0`)!
     - `check_item` sees `market_now == nil` and sets `markets[key] = nil`.
     - When the player buys the first `Lone Sword` at `300c` (where a second identical auction exists at `300c`) or buys 20 of the 80 `Dumped Ore`, `on_buy` / `on_success` (`tabs/sniper/core.lua:718, 760`) sets `known[key] = nil` so the next round will find the remaining units; or if the player adjusts the Sniper percent/profit boxes, `settings_changed()` wipes `known` and `markets`.
     - In the next round, `check_list` sees `markets[key] == nil`, calls `history.latest(price_key)`, gets `latest = 300` (or `210`) with `age = 0` from Round 1's own `scan.read_item`, sets `market = 300`, and rejects the deal (`100% of market`). Any new auction of that item listed at `300c` to `500c` later that day is also rejected without being opened.
  2. **The Full scan trade-off affects any item with up to 40 units (`1 at 3s, 39 at 32s`), not just a lone auction:**
     - In `core/history.lua:227`, `history.market_price` uses `MARKET_SHARE = .05` (`middle = ceil(max(1, ceil(total * 0.05)) / 2)`). For any `total` from `1` to `40`, `ceil(total * 0.05)` is `1` or `2`, so `middle == 1` (the single cheapest unit!).
     - Concrete listing: During today's Full scan, a gear item or recipe has **1 snipe at `3s` (`300`) and 39 normal auctions at `32s` (`3200`)** (`total = 40`). `history.market_price` sets `record.market = 300` (`3s`), so `check_list` treats `3s` as today's market and never offers the `3s` snipe.
     - And for an auction that truly is alone on the auction house (`result.totalQuantity == 1` and no other versions), the browse list already reports `result.totalQuantity == 1`, which can never be a flat market; not applying today's history ceiling when `result.totalQuantity <= 1` (or `today_units <= 1`) eliminates the lone-auction trade-off completely.
  3. **`refresh_deal` overwrites multi-version history for gear with an ending:** When `refresh_deal` (`tabs/sniper/core.lua:639-642`) re-reads a selected deal older than 15s, it calls `scan.start{queries = {{item_keys = {item_key}}}}`, which runs `scan_item_keys` (`core/scan.lua:549-579`) with a 1-key list and calls `history.write_view` for that single version, bypassing `one_version` in `record_view`.
  - **Fix:**
    - In `check_item`, store `markets[key] = {market = market or false, at = GetTime()}` so a fresh `memory` where `market_now` was `nil` (`memory.market == false`) is known to have no market wall and is not overridden by `history`.
    - In `scan.read_item` (or `record_view`), do not let a Sniper single-item read overwrite today's `record.market` when `market_now` is `nil` (or only use `history.today_market` in `check_list` when `record.market` exists, `record.units > 1`, and `result.totalQuantity > 1`, never falling back to `record.low`).
- **How sure:** Fact from the addon code (`tabs/sniper/core.lua`, `core/scan.lua`, `core/history.lua`).

### 3. `other_versions` in `check_list` and `market_now` ignores `totalQuantity`, `containsOwnerItem`, and `price <= cheapest`, both hiding real deals and letting false deals through

- **What:** `other_versions` (`auxForever/tabs/sniper/core.lua:326-339`) collects only `other.minPrice` from other browse rows sharing a `price_key`. In `check_list` (`core.lua:392-395`), every `other` price clamps `market = min(market, other)`. In `market_now` (`core.lua:131-134`), every `other` price is inserted into `others` with `units = 1` without checking `price > cheapest`.
- **Where:** `auxForever/tabs/sniper/core.lua:131-134, 326-363, 392-395`
- **Why it matters to a player:**
  1. **Hides real deals when two versions are both cheap or one version has a low starting bid:**
     - **Concrete listing A (two cheap versions):** `Invoker's Cord` (`usual = 32s` / `3200`) has 1 auction of Version 11 at `10s` (`result(901, 11, 1, 1000)`) and 1 auction of Version 12 at `11s` (`result(901, 12, 1, 1100)`), with nothing else listed. In `check_list`, Version 11 (`1000`) is judged against Version 12 (`market = 1100`, `91% > 60%`) and skipped; Version 12 (`1100`) is judged against Version 11 (`market = 1000`, `110% > 60%`) and skipped. Both are marked `known` and **neither is opened**.
     - **Concrete listing B (low starting bid on another version):** Version 11 has 1 auction with a `5s` starting bid and `32s` buyout (`result(901, 11, 1, 500)`, since `minPrice` on the browse list can be a bid, `[Code]`), and Version 12 has 1 auction with an `8s` buyout (`result(901, 12, 1, 800)`). In `check_list`, Version 11 (`500` vs `800` = `63%`) and Version 12 (`800` vs `500` = `160%`) block each other and neither opens. Even if Version 12 is `4s` (`400`, opened) and Version 11 has a `3s` starting bid (`300`), `market_now` inserts `{price = 300, units = 1}` into `others` (because line 131 does not check `price > cheapest`) and returns `market = 300`, rejecting the `4s` buyout deal because of another version's `3s` bid.
     - **Concrete listing C (player's own auction on another version):** The player has listed 1 `Invoker's Cord` (Version 12) at `13s` (`containsOwnerItem = true, totalQuantity = 1`), and another seller lists Version 11 at `12s` (`usual = 32s`). `versions` includes the player's own Version 12 listing and blocks the `12s` deal (unlike `market_now` line 118 and the Post tab's `skip_key` in H-004 note 8).
  2. **Lets false deals through when the opened version has several overpriced listings and another version has dozens of cheap ones:**
     - **Concrete listing D:** Version 11 has 1 auction at `6s` (`600`, or `minPrice = 600` bid with `1200` buyout) and **8 overpriced auctions at `32s` (`3200`)**; Version 12 has **78 auctions at `13s` (`result(901, 12, 78, 1300)`)**. When Version 11 is opened, `other_versions` passes `{1300}` as `units = 1` instead of `78`. In `market_now`, `others` has `{1300 x 1, 3200 x 8}` (`total = 9`, `middle = ceil(ceil(9 * 0.25) / 2) = 2`). The 8 overpriced auctions at `32s` outvote the 78 auctions at `13s`, `market_now` returns `3200` (`32s`), and a `12s` buyout on Version 11 is called a deal (`38% of usual`) right next to 78 listings at `13s`.
  - **Fix:** Skip browse entries where `result.containsOwnerItem and (result.totalQuantity or 0) <= 1`; pass `{price = other.minPrice, units = other.totalQuantity}` (or sum `totalQuantity` across versions); in `check_list`, only let `other_versions` clamp `market` when the other versions have enough combined units (e.g. `>= MARKET_SELLERS`, or `other.minPrice > price` with multiple units) so two single-unit deals do not cancel each other out; and in `market_now`, only add `other` entries with `other.price > cheapest` (or count them toward `sellers` when `other.price == cheapest`).
- **How sure:** Fact from the addon code (`tabs/sniper/core.lua:131-134, 326-363, 392-395`) and `BrowseResultInfo` (`[Code]`, `docs/forever-auction-house.md`, section 9).

### 4. `market_now` has no minimum unit or seller floor on `others`, hiding real deals whenever two cheap listings are 1 copper apart with nothing else listed

- **What:** In `auxForever/tabs/sniper/core.lua:116-148`, when 1 or 2 sellers list 2 (or 80) cheap units at the *same* price (`{rec(210, 80)}`), `total == 0` and `sellers < 3`, so `market_now` returns `nil` and the Sniper offers the deal against `usual`. But if 1 or 2 sellers list cheap units at two prices 1 copper apart with no higher listings, `unit > cheapest` puts the second price into `others` with `total >= 1`, and `market_now` returns that second cheap price as the market.
- **Where:** `auxForever/tabs/sniper/core.lua:116-148` (`M.market_now`)
- **Why it matters to a player:**
  - **Concrete listing:** An item with `usual = 22s` (`2200`) has only two cheap listings on the auction house: **1 unit at `2s 09c` (`209`) and 1 unit at `2s 10c` (`210`)** (`{rec(209, 1), rec(210, 1)}`, from the same seller or two sellers, with nothing else listed). `market_now` sets `cheapest = 209`, puts `{price = 210, units = 1}` into `others` (`total = 1`), and returns `market = 210`. `judge` sees `209 / 210 = 100% > 60%` and rejects the deal.
  - Yet if a third seller adds **100 units at `22s`** (`{rec(209, 1), rec(210, 1), rec(2200, 100)}`), `market_now` returns `2200` and the Sniper offers *both* the `2s 09c` and `2s 10c` units as deals! Having *less* supply on the auction house (only the 2 cheap units) hides the deal, while having *more* supply (102 units) shows it.
  - **Fix:** A single unit (or listings from fewer than `MARKET_SELLERS` total sellers across the item when total units are small) above `cheapest` is not a supply wall unless the total sellers across the cheapest quarter (including `cheapest`) is at least `MARKET_SELLERS` (3) or `total` above `cheapest` is a real stack of supply.
- **How sure:** Fact from the addon code (`tabs/sniper/core.lua:116-148`).

### 5. `markets[key]` stays at the old low price for 15 minutes even after `result.minPrice` proves the cheap wall sold out, and `known[key]` permanently locks out items rejected via `other_versions` or `markets[key]`

- **What:**
  1. In `auxForever/tabs/sniper/core.lua:386-387`, `markets[key]` is trusted for `MARKET_MEMORY` (15 minutes) without checking whether `result.minPrice` on the browse list has risen above `memory.market`.
  2. In `core.lua:369, 400`, when `judge` returns `nil` in `check_list` because of `markets[key]` or `other_versions`, `known[key] = price` is recorded. Subsequent rounds skip `key` whenever `known[key] == price`.
- **Where:** `auxForever/tabs/sniper/core.lua:278, 369, 386-401, 480-505`
- **Why it matters to a player:**
  1. **Stale `markets[key]` hides a real snipe after a buyout:** Suppose `Flat Herb` (`usual = 22s`) has 2 at `85c`, 45 at `87c`, and 100 at `22s`. Round 1 opens it and records `markets[key] = {market = 87, at = t0}`. One minute later, a crafter buys all the `85c` and `87c` herbs, so in Round 2 `result.minPrice` on the browse list is `2200` (`22s`). Round 2 sets `known[key] = 2200` and leaves `markets[key].market = 87` intact. Two minutes later, someone posts a real snipe at `55c` (`2.5%` of `22s`). Round 3 compares `55c` against the stale `markets[key].market = 87c` (`63% > 60%`), rejects the `55c` snipe, and sets `known[key] = 55`. Clearing `markets[key]` whenever `price > memory.market` (since `minPrice` is the lowest price on the whole auction house, no listings at `memory.market` remain) prevents this.
  2. **Permanent lockout in `known[key]`:** If Version 11 (`minPrice = 12s`, `usual = 32s`) is rejected in Round 1 only because Version 12 was momentarily listed at `13s` (or because of `markets[key]`), line 400 sets `known[ka] = 1200`. When Version 12 sells out in Round 2 (or when `markets[key]` expires after 15 minutes), Version 11 is still at `minPrice = 1200`, so `known[ka] ~= price` is false and Version 11 is never checked again.
  3. **Minor cleanup:** `M.clear_deals()`, `M.unignore_all()`, and `aux.event.CLOSE()` do not wipe `markets`, and `M.settings_changed()` does not clear `deal.deal_read_at` or re-filter `deal.deal_tiers` on already-found commodity deals.
- **How sure:** Fact from the addon code (`tabs/sniper/core.lua:278, 369, 386-401, 480-505`).

### 6. `FRESH_SECONDS` skips `refresh_deal` for gear even when another item or round was searched after `deal_read_at`

- **What:** In `auxForever/tabs/sniper/core.lua:629`, `refresh_deal` skips re-reading the item whenever `record.deal_read_at and GetTime() - record.deal_read_at <= FRESH_SECONDS` (`15` seconds), for both commodities and gear (`not record.commodity`).
- **Where:** `auxForever/tabs/sniper/core.lua:29, 303, 615-632`
- **Why it matters to a player:** In 0.4.1 (`[Seen]`, recorded in `tabs/sniper/core.lua:615-619`), gear deals could not be bought from the Sniper (clicking Buy did nothing), while trade goods from the Sniper could. The cause noted in `core.lua:616-618` (`[Unknown]` / inference) is that `C_AuctionHouse.PlaceBid(auctionID, buyoutAmount)` requires the auction's item to be the latest item searched, and the Sniper searches other candidates in the same round (`check_list` lines 411-414) or starts the next round 2.5 seconds later (`ROUND_PAUSE = 2.5`). Even in `test fb028` (`tests/load_test.lua:5065-5079`), the round searches `701` (`Quick Axe`, gear) and immediately searches `702` (`Quick Ore`) after it. If the player clicks `Quick Axe` 2 seconds later (or after the next round's browse/search query within 15 seconds), `refresh_deal` is skipped even though `701` is no longer the latest search.
  - **Fix:** For commodities (`record.commodity`), `FRESH_SECONDS = 15` is always safe (`StartCommoditiesPurchase` takes `itemID` and fetches a live server quote, `[Code]` and `[Seen]`). For gear (`not record.commodity`), only skip `refresh_deal` within `FRESH_SECONDS` when no other search or browse query has run since `record.deal_item_key` was read.
- **How sure:** Fact from the addon code (`tabs/sniper/core.lua:615-632`); whether `C_AuctionHouse.PlaceBid` on Forever requires the item to be the latest search or only that no new browse/search cleared the client's item results is `[Unknown]` (inferred from the 0.4.1 `[Seen]` failure).

### 7. When a deal is judged against `market` (`% of market`), the table still shows `deal_usual` in the `Usual` column and never shows `deal_market`

- **What:** In `auxForever/tabs/sniper/core.lua:83-90, 301-306`, when `market < usual`, `judge` sets `reason = 'market'` and computes `deal_percent` and `deal_profit` from `market`, while `cheapest.deal_usual` is still set to `usual`. In `auxForever/tabs/sniper/frame.lua:117-161`, the columns show `Price each`, `Usual` (`record.deal_usual`), `Why` (`30% of market`), and `Profit each` (`record.deal_profit`).
- **Where:**
  - `auxForever/tabs/sniper/core.lua:83-90, 301-306`
  - `auxForever/tabs/sniper/frame.lua:117-161`
- **Why it matters to a player:** In the `Fallen Cloth` test case (`Price each: 3s`, `usual = 22s`, `market = 10s`), the row reads:
  `Price each: 3s | Usual: 22s | Why: 30% of market | Profit each: 6s 50c`.
  The `10s` market price is not shown anywhere on the row, so the numbers on screen appear contradictory (`3s` is 14% of `22s`, not 30%, and 95% of `22s` minus `3s` is `17s 90c`, not `6s 50c`). Showing `record.deal_market` when `record.deal_reason == 'market'` (or showing the market price in the column/tooltip) lets the player see the reference price that `30% of market` and `Profit each` were calculated from.
- **How sure:** Fact from the addon code (`tabs/sniper/core.lua:301-306`, `tabs/sniper/frame.lua:117-161`).

### 8. The new `versions` pre-pass in `check_list` calls `info.item_key_price_key` on all ~7,700 items and adds 38 `coro_wait()` frame yields on every round

- **What:** In `auxForever/tabs/sniper/core.lua:345-363`, build 2 added a full pre-pass over `results` that calls `info.item_key_price_key(result.itemKey)` for every item on the auction house and yields every 200 items (`if i % 200 == 0 then aux.coro_wait() end`), before the main loop (`lines 364-408`) walks `results` a second time and calls `info.item_key_price_key` again.
- **Where:** `auxForever/tabs/sniper/core.lua:345-408` (`check_list`)
- **Why it matters to a player:**
  1. **Adds ~0.65 seconds (38 frame yields) to every Sniper round:** For 7,718 items (`[Seen]`, `docs/forever-auction-house.md`, section 9), yielding every 200 items in a second loop adds 38 extra frames of delay to every round before any candidate is opened.
  2. **Unnecessary per-round work and string/API calls:** In `auxForever/util/info.lua:531-560`, `price_key` always begins with `itemID .. ':'`. Two entries in `results` can *never* share a `price_key` unless they share `result.itemKey.itemID` (and have `(result.itemKey.itemSuffix or 0) ~= 0`). On rounds 2+, ~99% of items have `known[key] == price`, and ~80%+ of `itemID`s appear only once in `results`. Grouping by `result.itemKey.itemID` (a plain number) or only resolving `item_key_price_key` when an `itemID` has multiple entries and a candidate is being checked avoids thousands of `itemID .. ':0'` strings and `C_AuctionHouse.GetItemKeyInfo` calls every 2.5 seconds.
- **How sure:** Fact from the addon code (`tabs/sniper/core.lua:345-408`, `util/info.lua:531-560`).
