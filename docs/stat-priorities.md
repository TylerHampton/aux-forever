# Stat priorities for the Upgrades tab (WoW Forever)

The Upgrades tab (`docs/upgrades.md`) scores gear by each spec's stat priority. These are the
priorities from Icy Veins' WoW Forever stat pages, one per spec, read on 2026-11-16 (Ben chose the
source). They are written for Forever, not Classic: they use Forever's stats (Expertise, Haste,
Spell Pierce, Healing Power) and its caps (no guaranteed 1% spell miss). All are **[Outside]**:
a guide's advice, not game data.

Every page says the same things up front: it is written for level 60, it may change as Forever's
level 60 gear becomes known, and the amount of a stat matters as much as its place in the list. A
number in brackets is the page's cap for that stat against level 63 raid bosses.

Pages: `https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/<spec>-stat-priority`, where `<spec>` is the slug in
each heading below. Last updated as shown on each page, with its author.

## Druid

- **Balance** (`balance-druid-ranged-dps-pve`, Nov 4, Voulk): Hit Chance (17%), Critical Strike,
  Spell Power, Intellect. Also on the page: Haste, Spirit, MP5.
- **Feral, damage and threat** (`feral-druid-melee-dps-and-tank-pve`, Nov 4, Meyra): Weapon DPS,
  Expertise (6.5% for damage from behind, 14% for tanking), Hit Chance (8%), Agility, Strength.
  Critical Strike and Attack Power are worth about as much as Agility and Strength. Weapon speed
  does not matter (forms swing at a fixed speed), only weapon DPS.
- **Feral, tank survival** (same page): Armor, Defense (to 440), Stamina, Agility, Dodge.
- **Restoration** (`restoration-druid-healer-pve`, Nov 4, Voulk): Healing Power, Intellect,
  Spirit, MP5, Critical Strike. Healing Power items also give a third as much Spell Power.

## Hunter

- **Beast Mastery** (`beast-mastery-hunter-ranged-dps-pve`, Nov 4, Impakt): Weapon Damage, Hit
  Chance (8%), Critical Strike, Agility, Attack Power. Intellect gives Attack Power through the
  Careful Aim talent, so it is worth a little more than Attack Power. A slow weapon with high damage
  per shot beats one with higher DPS.
- **Marksmanship** (`marksmanship-hunter-ranged-dps-pve`, Nov 4, Impakt): the same list and notes.
- **Survival** (`survival-hunter-melee-dps-pve`, Nov 4, Veramos): Weapon Damage, Hit Chance (8%;
  5% from gear with Surefooted), Critical Strike, Agility, Attack Power.

## Mage

- **Arcane** (`arcane-mage-ranged-dps-pve`, Nov 4, Farmerfarms): Hit Chance (17%), Spell Power,
  Critical Strike, Intellect, Spirit, MP5.
- **Fire** (`fire-mage-ranged-dps-pve`, Nov 4, Farmerfarms): the same list.
- **Frost** (`frost-mage-ranged-dps-pve`, Nov 4, Teloril): Hit Chance (17%), Spell Power, Critical
  Strike, Intellect, MP5, Spirit.

## Paladin

- **Retribution, Unsealed Wrath build** (`retribution-paladin-melee-dps-pve`, Nov 4, Meyra): Weapon
  Damage, Hit Chance (8%), Expertise (6.5%), Strength, Spell Power. Two-handed weapons.
- **Retribution, Holy Shock build** (same page): Weapon DPS, Hit Chance (8%), Spell Power, Critical
  Strike, Expertise (6.5%). Prefers a faster two-hander, even at slightly lower DPS.
- **Protection** (`protection-paladin-tank-pve`, Nov 4, Mytholxgy): Stamina, Defense (to 440),
  Spell Power, Intellect, Strength.
- **Holy** (`holy-paladin-healer-pve`, Nov 4, Mytholxgy): Healing Power, Intellect, Spirit, MP5,
  Critical Strike.

## Priest

- **Discipline** (`discipline-priest-healer-pve`, Nov 4, Rainy): Healing Power, MP5, Spirit,
  Intellect, Critical Strike.
- **Holy** (`holy-priest-healer-pve`, Nov 4, Rainy): the same list.
- **Shadow** (`shadow-priest-ranged-dps-pve`, Nov 4, Anshlun): Hit Chance (17%; 12% from gear with
  Shadow Focus), Spell Power, Critical Strike, Spell Pierce, Spirit.

## Rogue

- **Assassination** (`assassination-rogue-melee-dps-pve`, Nov 4, Sellin): Expertise (6.5%), Hit
  Chance (8%), Agility, Weapon Damage, Critical Strike. Two daggers (Mutilate).
- **Combat** (`combat-rogue-melee-dps-pve`, Nov 4, Sellin): the same list.
- **Subtlety** (`subtlety-rogue-melee-dps-pve`, Nov 4, Seliathan): Weapon Damage, Expertise (6.5%),
  Hit Chance (8%), Weapon Skill, Agility.

## Shaman

- **Elemental** (`elemental-shaman-ranged-dps-pve`, Nov 4, Seksixeny): Spell Power, Hit Chance
  (17%), Critical Strike, Intellect, MP5.
- **Enhancement** (`enhancement-shaman-melee-dps-pve`, Nov 13, Wordup): Weapon DPS, Hit Chance (8%
  for melee), Expertise, Strength, Intellect, Spell Power. Critical Strike is also good, Agility
  only as an extra. Slow two-handers (3.3 speed or slower). Updated after the November 12 hotfixes
  to Mental Dexterity; Spell Pierce and Haste not judged yet.
- **Restoration** (`restoration-shaman-healer-pve`, Nov 4, Seksixeny): Healing Power, Critical
  Strike, Intellect, Spirit, MP5.

## Warlock

- **Affliction** (`affliction-warlock-ranged-dps-pve`, Nov 4, Abide): Hit Chance (17% with
  talents), Spell Power, Critical Strike, Haste, Intellect.
- **Demonology** (`demonology-warlock-ranged-dps-pve`, Nov 4, Abide): the same list.
- **Destruction** (`destruction-warlock-ranged-dps-pve`, Nov 4, Abide): Hit Chance (17% with
  talents), Spell Power, Haste, Critical Strike, Intellect.

## Warrior

- **Arms** (`arms-warrior-melee-dps-pve`, Nov 4, Abide): Weapon DPS, Hit Chance (8% with a
  two-hander), Expertise (6.5% from behind), Critical Strike, Strength. Haste, Agility and Attack
  Power still count.
- **Fury** (`fury-warrior-melee-dps-pve`, Nov 4, Abide): the same list; 27% hit for dual-wield
  auto attacks before talents.
- **Protection, threat** (`protection-warrior-tank-pve`, Nov 4, Abide): Expertise, Hit Chance,
  Agility, Strength, Stamina.
- **Protection, survival** (same page): Stamina, Armor, Agility, Avoidance, Defense.

## Not in these lists

The pages give an order, not numbers per point, except a few conversions (Enhancement: 1 Strength
is 2 Attack Power, 14 Attack Power is 1 weapon DPS). How an order becomes weights is decided in
`docs/upgrades.md`.
