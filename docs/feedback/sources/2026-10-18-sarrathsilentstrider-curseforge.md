tyehampton, Author (1 day ago):
@sarrathsilentstrider I was not under the impression that my add-on limits the price you can post at to be exactly at the lowest cost or 1c(commodities)/1s(gear/weapons) below lowest cost. Are you not able to edit the post price to be whatever price you want?

Also, whether you are undercut by 1c or 1g, people undercut for a single reason... to put their listing in 1st place so it sells first. If you are undercut by 59c, you response is to undercut by 1c again. The goal, at least for me, is to make a much money as possible AND have my item sell first. If some clown wants to undercut by more than the minimum that is their problem, when we want to undercut we almost always do it for the minimum except in the rare circumstance when we believe that the price is to inflated to sell before we are undercut via time or cost, in which case we choose our undercut with a manually typed price.

You can post at any price you like. Click the Buyout box on the Post tab and type the price, for example 12g 34s 50c. A typed price replaces the automatic one. You can also click any row in the listings to post at that row's price. The undercut switch only sets the starting point, one step under the lowest. Wait for the listings to finish loading before you type, or the lowest price can replace what you typed. That part is a bug we're fixing.

With all this being said, do you still believe that auxForever should have expanded functionality around undercutting, even though it is much less effective in this version of the game?
